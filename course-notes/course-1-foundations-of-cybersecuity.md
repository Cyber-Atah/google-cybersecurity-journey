Key Concepts

1. What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting networks, devices, and data from unauthorized access and attacks while ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

- Confidentiality: Information is accessible only to authorized people.
- Integrity: Information remains accurate and is not changed without authorization.
- Availability: Information and systems are accessible when needed.

2. Role of a Cybersecurity Analyst

A cybersecurity analyst helps protect an organization's networks, devices, and data from unauthorized access and cyber threats. They monitor systems, investigate suspicious activities, identify security risks, and help keep information secure.
